# 8-Ball

8-ball pool for two players, played by passing links in chat. No accounts, no server, no sign-in.

Play: https://john-gustin.github.io/8-ball/

1. Open the page, enter your names, and break.
2. When your turn ends, copy the link and paste it into your chat.
3. Your friend opens it, watches your shots replay, takes their turn, and sends a link back.

The whole game state lives in the link (after the `#`), so nothing is stored anywhere else.
