8-Ball with a friend

Take your shots, then send your friend a link. They open it (no sign-in needed), watch your shots replay, and take theirs.

1. ShootAim with the mouse. Press and pull back to set power, then release.
2. Send the linkWhen your turn ends, copy the link and paste it into Google Chat.
3. RepeatYour friend shoots and sends a link back. First to sink the 8 legally wins.
